During the day we have different energy levels. It would make sense to plan our
daily activities while taking those different energy levels in mind:

| Phase      | Amount of hours | Timetable     | What to do?                             | Category |
|------------|-----------------|---------------|-----------------------------------------|----------|
| Activation | 1 - 2           | 07:00 - 09:00 | Simple tasks, almost no decision-making | Care     |
| Focus      | 4 - 6           | 09:00 - 14:00 | Most cognitive demanding work (think)   | Opus     |
| Connection | 4 - 5           | 14:00 - 19:00 | Social and emotional work               | Amor     |
| Reflection | 4 - 5           | 19:00 - 23:00 | Creative work                           | Ludere   |
